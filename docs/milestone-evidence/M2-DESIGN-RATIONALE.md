# M2 design rationale

## Borrowed

- **Stanford HCI:** clear primary navigation, research discoverability, restrained chrome.
- **MIT HCI Engineering:** artifact-first storytelling; featured work before organizational filler.
- **John Horton:** publication-like typography and quiet metadata hierarchy (year/venue rhythm later in M3).
- **Transitions.dev / MotionSites:** small state/affordance motion only (link gap, hover color); no scroll-jacking.

## Retained from our concept

Warm off-white (`#F8F7F3`), deep ink, muted teal accent, Georgia display + Source Sans body, “Intelligence is an interaction.” hero, overlapping-circle mark, editorial section numbering.

## Deliberately avoided

Corporate AI landing aesthetics, purple gradients, carousels, oversized marketing slogans, dark-mode-first skins, invented publication titles/awards, stock/AI “research” photos presented as real studies.

## Content honesty

Featured research and news modules use explicit **development placeholders** until M3 verification. Navigation exposes only implemented routes (Research, Publications, People) plus mailto contact.
