  Jainendra Shukla founded and directs the Human–Machine Interaction Research Group at IIIT-Delhi. His research investigates interactive intelligence: how intelligent systems perceive and understand people and their environments, learn through interaction, and adapt to changing human contexts.

  Working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human–computer interaction, affective computing, and social robotics, his work explores interactive technologies with applications in health, neurodevelopment, accessibility, and education.
